📋 OVERVIEW — July 31, 2026

Snook, redfish, and juvenile tarpon are firing across Marco Island's mangroves and passes, with topwater bites at first light giving way to steady live-bait action on the moving tides. Nearshore hard bottom continues to produce mangrove snapper, tripletail, and the odd goliath grouper — calm mornings, light traffic, and that tropical green-blue water are the story of the week.

Inshore

▼
Snook🔥 HOT
Spawning aggregations stacked on Big Marco and Caxambas passes — flair-hawks after dark, topwater plugs at first light along the outer mangrove points.
Redfish🔥 HOT
Tight to the mangrove roots and shell bars on high water — weedless gold spoons and live pilchards. Last two hours of the incoming is the money window.
Speckled Trout✓ GOOD
Deeper grass edges in 4-6 feet — shrimp under a popping cork keeps the numbers steady. Larger gators on topwater at dawn.
Tripletail✓ GOOD
Sitting on channel markers and crab-trap floats just outside the passes — free-lined live shrimp on the first cast.

Nearshore ≤5mi

▼
Mangrove Snapper🔥 HOT
Stacked on nearshore ledges and small wrecks — cut threadfin or live pilchards on light fluoro leader. Keepers to 16 inches steady.
Goliath Grouper✓ GOOD
Bruisers parked on shallow structure under five miles — heavy tackle, big cut bait, quick photo and release.
King Mackerel✓ GOOD
Trolled spoons and ribbons around bait pods on the outer edge — watch the birds and be ready for a screaming reel.
Spanish Mackerel· SLOW
Scattered — occasional bursts on jigs and Gotcha plugs when the bait shows, but no consistent schools right now.

Backwater

▼
Juvenile Tarpon🔥 HOT
Rolling in deep creek bends and dead-end bays through the Ten Thousand Islands — DOA Baitbusters, small swimbaits, or a well-placed baitfish fly. 20-50 pound class.
Snook (backcountry)✓ GOOD
Small-to-mid slot fish tucked deep in the mangroves — flip weedless jerkbaits into the shade pockets on the falling tide.
Jack Crevalle✓ GOOD
Blitzing bait schools in the open bays — topwater walking baits and gold spoons draw explosive strikes.
Sheepshead· SLOW
Off-season lull — a few holdovers around dock pilings on live shrimp, but they're the exception, not the rule.
