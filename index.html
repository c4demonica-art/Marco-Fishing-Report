📋 OVERVIEW — July 3, 2026

Summer fishing around Marco Island is firing on all cylinders this week with snook, redfish, and lingering tarpon leading the inshore action. Early mornings before the afternoon storms are the golden window — expect hot bites at dawn, then plan to be off the water by 2pm as the July heat and thunderheads build.

Inshore

▼
Snook🔥 HOT
Stacked in the passes and along beach dropoffs. Live pilchards, threadfin herring, and white paddletails on the moving tide.
Redfish✓ GOOD
Tailing on lower tides around oyster bars. Cut ladyfish, gold spoons, and shrimp under popping corks working well.
Trout✓ GOOD
Steady action on jigs and Gulp shrimp in 3-6 feet along grass edges early morning.

Nearshore ≤5mi

▼
Tarpon🔥 HOT
Migration tapering but still rolling along the beaches at first light. Live crabs during outgoing tide, pinfish free-lined on incoming.
Spanish Mackerel✓ GOOD
Trolling silver spoons and Clark spoons over nearshore bait pods. Sharks working the same schools.
Mangrove Snapper✓ GOOD
Anchored on hard bottom in 15-25 feet. Cut sardines and shrimp on light fluorocarbon leaders.

Backwater

▼
Redfish🔥 HOT
Ten Thousand Islands producing tailing reds on low tides. Fly and light spin both working — copper baitfish flies and weedless jerkbaits.
Snook✓ GOOD
Tucked under mangrove overhangs and along shaded creek edges. Skip-cast soft plastics and live shrimp.
Sheepshead· SLOW
Summer lull continues around dock pilings and structure. Better options elsewhere until fall.
