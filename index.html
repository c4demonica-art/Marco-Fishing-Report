📋 OVERVIEW — May 1, 2026

May kicks off the most exciting stretch of the year on Marco — the first big push of migrating tarpon is rolling the outside points and Ten Thousand Islands while pre-spawn snook stack the passes and outer islands. Warming water and returning bait have inshore action firing on all cylinders, with redfish, trout, and Spanish mackerel rounding out a complete week of options.

Inshore

▼
Snook🔥 HOT
Pre-spawn fish staging at Caxambas and outer mangrove points. Live whitebait or DOA shrimp on outgoing tides — biggest fish at first light.
Redfish🔥 HOT
Peak month for sight-fishing slot reds across Rookery Bay and Marco River flats. Gold spoons and shrimp flies on falling tides over sand holes.
Speckled Trout✓ GOOD
Steady action over grass flats in 3–5 ft. Popping corks with live shrimp; mornings best before the wind builds.

Nearshore

▼
Tarpon🔥 HOT
Migration officially on — fish rolling outside points and along the beaches south of Marco. Threadfins, crabs, or DOA Bait Busters on the strong tides.
Spanish Mackerel✓ GOOD
Schools busting glass minnows just outside the passes. Silver spoons or Gotcha plugs on long casts; wire leader saves rigs.
Sharks✓ GOOD
Blacktips, bonnetheads, and the occasional hammerhead working the passes. Cut ladyfish or jack on heavy spinning gear.

Backwater

▼
Laid-up Tarpon✓ GOOD
Back bays of the 10k holding daytime fish on calm mornings. Slow-stripped dark flies — patience and a quiet poling skiff are the difference.
Mangrove Snapper✓ GOOD
Solid numbers around oyster bars and dock pilings up the Marco River. Live shrimp on light fluorocarbon during moving water.
Pompano· SLOW
Tail end of the run — a few fish still showing on sandy edges but most have moved on. Small jigs tipped with shrimp if you target them.
