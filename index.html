📋 OVERVIEW — July 17, 2026

Full summer heat has Marco Island waters fired up early and slow by mid-day, with snook, redfish, trout, and juvenile tarpon leading the inshore charge on live bait and cut chunks. Fish the sunrise tides, pack extra water, and be off the flats before the afternoon thunderstorms roll in.

Inshore

▼
Snook🔥 HOT
Peak time for slob snook along the beaches, passes, and bay edges. Live pilchards and threadfins at first light — spoons and flies working too.
Redfish✓ GOOD
Slot reds on the oyster bars during the lower tides. Cut ladyfish or gold spoons worked quietly along the shell.
Speckled Trout✓ GOOD
Deeper grass edges holding fish. Popping cork with a live shrimp on the outgoing tide is the ticket.

Nearshore ≤5mi

▼
Tarpon✓ GOOD
Migration tapering but resident fun-sized silver kings still rolling the beaches early. Live crabs and threadfin herring on light leader.
Goliath Grouper🔥 HOT
Stacked on the wrecks and reefs. Big cut baits on heavy tackle — expect to lose one to the rocks before you land one.
Sharks🔥 HOT
Blacktips and bulls thick right off the beach. Freshly cut mackerel or ladyfish on a wire leader — hold on.

Backwater

▼
Sheepshead✓ GOOD
Still hanging around dock pilings and pass structure. Small live shrimp on a knocker rig — feel that soft chew and swing.
Black Drum✓ GOOD
Working the pass structure on the outgoing tide. Fresh shrimp on the bottom near shell bars.
Jack Crevalle🔥 HOT
Big schools tearing up bait pods in the back bays. Any topwater lure through the boil — total chaos, big fun.
