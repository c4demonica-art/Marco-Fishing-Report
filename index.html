📋 OVERVIEW — October 2, 2026

October is hitting the reset button on Marco Island fishing — cooler water temps have redfish spreading onto the flats, snook staging for their fall feed, and tarpon stacking in the passes for the mullet run. Variety is the headline this week, with inshore, nearshore, and backwater all producing on multiple species.

Inshore

▼
Redfish🔥 HOT
Schools pushing bait on flats and oyster bars. Overslot fish mixed in. Gold spoons, weightless shrimp, cut mullet.
Snook🔥 HOT
Fall harvest window open. Oyster points and dock lights firing on falling tide. Live pilchards and swimbaits.
Speckled Trout✓ GOOD
Returning to grass edges in 3-5 feet. Popping corks with Gulp shrimp producing steady limits.

Nearshore ≤5mi

▼
Spanish Mackerel🔥 HOT
Schools blowing up bait on artificial reefs. Silver spoons on fast retrieve, Gotcha plugs cleaning up.
Mangrove Snapper✓ GOOD
Steady on structure and wrecks. Live shrimp or small pinfish on light fluorocarbon. Keeper sizes mixed in.
King Mackerel✓ GOOD
Season kicking off. Slow-trolled live baits or spoons on planers out past the markers.

Backwater

▼
Tarpon🔥 HOT
Fall migration on. 40-pound class fish crushing mullet in passes at dawn. DOA Baitbusters, big swimbaits.
Snook✓ GOOD
Backcountry rivers steady on outgoing tide. Jerkbaits and live shrimp around mangrove points.
Jack Crevalle✓ GOOD
Running with tarpon in the passes. Topwater plugs for explosive strikes. Great kids' fish.
