📋 OVERVIEW — November 6, 2026

Fall patterns are locked in across Marco Island and the Ten Thousand Islands, with cooler mornings concentrating redfish and snook tight along backcountry oyster bars, mangrove points, and creek mouths. Juvenile tarpon are still rolling in the deeper passes and channels, while nearshore reefs and wrecks are loaded with Spanish mackerel, mangrove snapper, and the occasional king on the troll.

INSHORE

▼
Redfish🔥 HOT
Schooling on flood tides over oyster bars and against mangrove shorelines. Cut ladyfish, gold spoons, and weedless jerkbaits all producing. Best windows: first two hours of outgoing and last two of incoming.
Snook🔥 HOT
Thick in the creeks and on points. Live pinfish and shrimp on 20 lb fluoro leader is the ticket. Low light hours are on fire — topwater early then switch to subsurface as the sun climbs.
Sea Trout✓ GOOD
Keeper trout rebuilding over the grass flats in 3–5 feet. DOA shrimp under a popping cork, or paddletails on 1/8 oz jigheads.
Sheepshead✓ GOOD
First real push arriving on dock pilings, bridges, and seawalls. Fiddler crabs beat shrimp when you can get them.

NEARSHORE (≤5mi)

▼
Spanish Mackerel🔥 HOT
Blitzing bait schools just off the beaches. Silver spoons, gotcha plugs, and small jigs on wire leader. Birds will find them for you.
Mangrove Snapper✓ GOOD
Holding on nearshore reefs and ledges in 20–35 feet. Live shrimp or cut sardines on light fluoro. Chumming turns it on.
King Mackerel✓ GOOD
A few kings showing on the slow troll with blue runners and ribbonfish along 30–40 ft contour. Numbers improving with each cold front.
Gag Grouper· SLOW
Closed to harvest in state waters through year end — release only. Fish are around on hard bottom but handle with care.

BACKWATER

▼
Juvenile Tarpon✓ GOOD
20–50 lb poons rolling in the deeper channels and passes on tide changes. Live threadfins free-lined or DOA Baitbuster worked slow.
Black Drum✓ GOOD
Overslot drum under bridges and around deeper structure. Half a blue crab on a 4/0 circle hook, dead-stick it.
Jack Crevalle🔥 HOT
Blowing up mullet schools everywhere from the river mouths to the back creeks. Topwater plugs and bucktail jigs get crushed.
Ladyfish🔥 HOT
Everywhere. Non-stop action on jigs and small spoons — great for kids and perfect cut bait for reds.
