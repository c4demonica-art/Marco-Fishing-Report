📋 OVERVIEW — September 25, 2026

Late September has Marco Island firing on all cylinders — overslot redfish are schooling on the flats, big linesider snook are stacked at the passes and beach wash, and 40-pound-class tarpon continue to roll through Capri and Big Marco. Gulf water stays gin-clear ahead of the first fronts, backwater trout and mangrove snapper are steady, and tripletail on the markers round out productive mixed-bag days.

Inshore

▼
Snook🔥 HOT
Beach and pass snook peaking — live threadfins, pilchards, and white bucktails in the wash. Outgoing tide at dawn and dusk is money.
Redfish🔥 HOT
Overslot schools tailing on flats around Marco and Rookery Bay. Cut ladyfish, DOA shrimp under a popping cork, and gold spoons all producing.
Spotted Seatrout✓ GOOD
Steady action on grass flats in 3–5 feet. Live shrimp under a cork or 1/8 oz soft plastics through morning tides.

Nearshore ≤5mi

▼
Tarpon🔥 HOT
40-lb-class juveniles rolling Capri Pass and Big Marco. Live pinfish free-lined or fly patterns on floating lines at first light.
Mangrove Snapper✓ GOOD
Reliable limits on wrecks and ledges in 25–40 ft. Cut sardines and small live pilchards on 20-lb fluoro leader.
Goliath Grouper✓ GOOD
Big fish holding on nearshore wrecks and structure. Live blue runners and ladyfish on heavy tackle — catch, photo, quick release.

Backwater

▼
Redfish🔥 HOT
Ten Thousand Islands schools tailing oyster edges and pothole flats through the low. Weedless soft plastics and gold spoons.
Tripletail✓ GOOD
Sitting on channel markers and floating debris. Live shrimp pitched on 1/16 oz jighead — stealth approach is key.
Sheepshead· SLOW
Still warm for a real bite; a few fish around dock pilings on live shrimp and fiddler crabs. Numbers pick up with the first cool front.
