📋 OVERVIEW — June 26, 2026

Late-June backcountry is firing on every cylinder — tarpon, snook, redfish, trout, and tripletail are all eating, with the spawn run pushing the bigger snook out to the outside points and the migration tarpon still rolling along the beaches. Plan around the early bite before the afternoon thunderstorms shut the surface action down, and key on the moving tides at the passes for the most consistent shots.

Inshore

▼
Snook🔥 HOT
Spawning aggregations stacked on the passes and outside points. Topwater plugs and live pilchards on the first two hours of light, outgoing tide best — catch-and-release season, handle them quick.
Redfish🔥 HOT
Big over-slot fish hugging mangrove shade and oyster bars on the bottom of the tide. Cut pinfish, weedless gold spoons, and shrimp-pattern flies are all producing.
Sea Trout✓ GOOD
Solid numbers on the grass flats three to five feet early. Popping cork with live shrimp or paddle-tails on a 1/8 oz jighead through the tide change.

Nearshore ≤5mi

▼
Tarpon🔥 HOT
Migration schools still strung along the beaches from Cape Romano north. Live threadfins and crabs on the falling tide, sight-cast on the rollers before the wind builds.
Tripletail✓ GOOD
Hanging on crab trap floats and channel markers one to four miles out. Live shrimp on a small jighead pitched just ahead of the buoy.
Spanish Mackerel✓ GOOD
Busting bait pods on calm mornings. Silver spoons or Got-Cha plugs on a fast retrieve with light wire leader.
Mangrove Snapper· SLOW
Decent on the nearshore reefs but the bite has been short-windowed with the heat. Live shrimp on light fluoro around structure.

Backwater

▼
Redfish🔥 HOT
Ten Thousand Islands tight to the roots on low water. Fly anglers crushing it with shrimp patterns on 8-wt gear — sneak in quiet and pitch the shade.
Snook✓ GOOD
Smaller resident fish in the creek mouths and on the points. Subsurface twitchbaits at first light, then switch to soft plastics once the sun is up.
Black Drum✓ GOOD
Steady on the deeper holes near oyster bars. Half a blue crab on a fishfinder rig is dynamite.
Sheepshead· SLOW
Off-season — scattered fish around dock pilings and bridge structure. Live shrimp on a small jighead if you must.
