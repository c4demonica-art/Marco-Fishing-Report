📋 OVERVIEW — October 23, 2026

Late October has cooled the water across Marco Island, Goodland, and the Ten Thousand Islands, triggering an active fall transition bite with snook staging around passes, redfish tailing on the grass flats, and bait pods pushing nearshore structure. Mornings are calm and clear, afternoons breezy from the east, with a few scattered juvenile tarpon still in the backcountry creeks.

Inshore

▼
Snook🔥 HOT
Pass staging and dock lights firing. Live mullet, flare hawks after dark, and topwater at first light. Big females still around the mouths.
Redfish🔥 HOT
Schooled on shallow grass and oyster bars. Cut ladyfish and gold spoons. Tailing in the first hour of daylight on a slow outgoing tide.
Spotted Seatrout✓ GOOD
Cooler water has them active across Johnson Bay and Rookery Bay flats. Shrimp under popping corks, soft plastics mid-column.
Jack Crevalle✓ GOOD
Running mullet pods aggressively. Topwater plugs and large swimbaits during the morning blitzes.

Nearshore (≤5mi)

▼
Spanish Mackerel🔥 HOT
Thick on 3-5mi numbers. Fast-trolled spoons, silver gotchas, and bait-ball runoffs. Watch the bird play.
Kingfish✓ GOOD
Slow-trolled threadfins and blue runners over the hard bottom. Scattered keepers mixed with juveniles.
Mangrove Snapper✓ GOOD
Still holding on the artificial reefs. Cut sardines and small live shrimp on knocker rigs.
Permit· SLOW
A few sightings on the wrecks but the window is closing as water cools. Live crabs if you find them.

Backwater

▼
Snook🔥 HOT
Deep creek bends and residential dock lights after dark. White flare hawks, DOA shrimp, live pilchards along the mangroves.
Redfish🔥 HOT
Pushed up tight to the mangroves on high water, bars on the fall. Weedless gold spoons and cut mullet.
Juvenile Tarpon✓ GOOD
Still rolling in the back of Caxambas and the Ten Thousand Islands creeks at dawn. Baitbusters and small threadfin flies.
Sheepshead· SLOW
Starting to show on dock pilings. Live shrimp on light jigheads. Numbers should climb as water keeps cooling.
