📋 OVERVIEW — October 16, 2026

Fall patterns are settling in across Marco Island and the Ten Thousand Islands as cooler mornings push bait south along the beaches and into the backcountry creeks. Snook and redfish continue to lead the inshore scorecard, trout action is building on the deeper grass flats, and the resident tarpon are still rolling in the backwater bays before the first real cold front shuts them down.

Inshore

▼
Snook🔥 HOT
Keeper-slot fish feeding hard along mangrove points and beach cuts on moving tides. Live pilchards and shiners are the top baits; jerk shads work when bait is scarce.
Redfish🔥 HOT
Overslot reds pushing 28 inches on the oyster bars at low water. Soft plastics worked slow or live shrimp under popping corks are both producing.
Spotted Seatrout✓ GOOD
Deeper grass flats in 4–6 ft of water. Popping corks with live shrimp or DOA Deadly Combos — steady morning bite.
Sheepshead✓ GOOD
Starting to stack up on the docks, bridges and residential canals. Fiddler crabs and shrimp pieces on a split-shot rig.

Nearshore (≤5 mi)

▼
Mangrove Snapper🔥 HOT
Artificial reefs within 5 miles are loaded. Small live shrimp or cut sardine on knocker rigs with 20 lb fluoro.
Spanish Mackerel✓ GOOD
Schooling over the shallow wrecks and chasing glass minnows. Silver spoons and Gotcha plugs on a fast retrieve.
Gag Grouper✓ GOOD
Season open — hitting live pinfish and large shrimp on the ledges in 25–40 ft. Heavy leader and quick-pull technique.
Kingfish· SLOW
A few scattered fish but no consistent bite yet; waiting on the big bait schools to stack along the beaches.

Backwater

▼
Juvenile Tarpon🔥 HOT
20–50 lb fish rolling at daybreak in the deeper creek mouths and backcountry bays. DOA shrimp or small white flies — be on the water by 7 AM.
Snook🔥 HOT
Deep bends in the creeks hold stacked fish on the falling tide. Twitchbaits, paddletails and small live baits all drawing bites.
Jack Crevalle✓ GOOD
Busting bait on top from mid-morning on. Topwater plugs and gold spoons for arm-burning fights on light tackle.
Black Drum✓ GOOD
Oyster bars inside the islands at the bottom of the tide. Cut shrimp on light jig heads worked slow along the shells.
