📋 OVERVIEW — September 18, 2026

September is delivering on its reputation as one of Marco Island's premier fishing months, with overslot redfish schooling on the flats, big linesider snook holding around the passes and beaches, and 40-pound-class tarpon rolling through Capri and Big Marco. Gin-clear Gulf water is fueling exceptional sight-fishing along the beaches, while backwater trout, mangrove snapper, and tripletail are rounding out productive mixed-bag days.

Inshore

▼
Snook🔥 HOT
Beach and pass snook are at peak — live threadfins, pilchards, and white bucktails on the beach wash. Best on outgoing tide, dawn and dusk.
Redfish🔥 HOT
Overslot reds schooling on flats around Marco and Rookery Bay. Cut ladyfish, DOA shrimp under popping cork, and gold spoons producing.
Spotted Seatrout✓ GOOD
Consistent action on grass flats in 3–5 feet. Live shrimp under a cork or soft plastics on 1/8 oz jigheads through morning tides.

Nearshore ≤5mi

▼
Tarpon🔥 HOT
40-lb-class juveniles rolling around Capri Pass and Big Marco. Live pinfish free-lined or fly patterns on floating lines at first light.
Mangrove Snapper✓ GOOD
Reliable limits on nearshore wrecks and ledges in 25–40 ft. Cut sardines and small live pilchards on 20-lb fluoro leader.
Goliath Grouper✓ GOOD
Big fish holding on nearshore wrecks and structure. Live blue runners and ladyfish on heavy tackle — catch, photo, quick release.

Backwater

▼
Redfish🔥 HOT
Ten Thousand Islands schools of tailing reds on oyster edges and pothole flats through the low tide. Weedless soft plastics and gold spoons.
Tripletail✓ GOOD
Sitting on channel markers and floating debris. Live shrimp pitched on 1/16 oz jighead — a stealth approach is key.
Sheepshead· SLOW
Still warm for consistent bite; a few fish around dock pilings on live shrimp and fiddler crabs. Better numbers arrive with the first cool front.
