📋 OVERVIEW — August 21, 2026

Marco Island is squarely in mid-summer mode this week — warm gulf water, near-daily afternoon thunderstorms, and an inshore bite that rewards anglers who start at first light. Mangrove snapper, redfish tucked in the shade, and released snook off creek mouths are leading the way, with a few late-run tarpon still cruising the beaches on calm mornings.

Inshore

▼
Snook🔥 HOT
Stacked on creek mouths and mangrove points at first light. Closed season — catch and release only. Live pilchards or a white paddletail worked slow along the shade line.
Redfish✓ GOOD
Pushed off the open flats into shaded creek mouths and deeper backcountry pockets during mid-day heat. Cut bait on the bottom or a gold spoon on the higher tide.
Speckled Trout✓ GOOD
Best on grass flats in 3-5ft at daylight — popping cork with live shrimp. Bite slows fast as the sun climbs and water pushes past 88°F.

Nearshore (≤5mi)

▼
Mangrove Snapper🔥 HOT
Most reliable fish going right now — piled on nearshore wrecks, ledges and reefs. Small live shrimp or cut sardines on 20lb fluoro, dropped to bottom.
Spanish Mackerel✓ GOOD
Chasing bait pods just off the beach and around Big Marco Pass. Fast-retrieved spoons or a long shank Gotcha on light wire.
Tarpon· SLOW
Late-summer straggler bite — a few fish still rolling beach mornings, but the main run has passed. Live threadfin or a big soft plastic on calm days.

Backwater

▼
Tripletail✓ GOOD
Sitting on stone crab buoys and channel markers in the passes. Sight-cast a live shrimp under a small float — pitch past and drift it back.
Sharks🔥 HOT
Blacktips and bonnetheads busting bait pods in the passes and skinny water alike. Ladyfish chunks on wire — hold on.
Jack Crevalle✓ GOOD
Roving packs busting glass minnows against the mangrove points on the outgoing. Topwater walker or a swimbait fished fast.
